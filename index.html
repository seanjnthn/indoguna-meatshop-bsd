About

A steakhouse that grew out of a butcher's passion.

At The Icon, BSD City, Indoguna Meatshop & Gourmet brings two experiences under one roof: a relaxed restaurant serving freshly grilled steaks, and a full gourmet counter where every cut on your plate is also yours to take home.

Backed by Indoguna's long-standing food-supply heritage, the kitchen and the butchery work side by side — so steaks arrive at your table at their freshest, alongside sausages, cold cuts, seafood, dairy and imported seasonings.

Guests love that you can eat well and shop well in one visit — pick your cut at the display, have it cooked to order, then stock up for your own kitchen before you leave.
